Research projects

EvoMaxx v10: an artificial life sandbox on the GPU2026, ongoing

WebGL2 system in which an eight-gene regulatory network drives the red, green and blue channels of every pixel, and the world generates its own music. Mapped a 24,480-rule space with controlled experiments on about 18,000 worlds; raised the share of lifelike genomes from 7% to 95%. Reproduced twelve papers it builds on (Lenia, Flow-Lenia, Physarum, MAP-Elites and others).

Slavemaker Swarm: individual-based model of dulosis2026

3-D agent-based simulation of Polyergus raids with five pheromone fields, an enslavement pipeline, queen usurpation, slave rebellion and colony-level coevolution.

AEASS v3: memetic algorithm for constraint satisfaction2025; revised 2026

GA with Lamarckian min-conflicts local search and ILS escapes on logic-reduced domains, in Python, C99 and WebGL2. 65,201 of 65,201 verified runs solved over 52,458 benchmark puzzles, with a full ablation.
